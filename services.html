Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website? ▼
Starter websites are delivered in 3-5 business days. Business websites take 5-7 days. Full stack apps and custom projects take 2-4 weeks depending on complexity. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
What do you need from me to get started? ▼
Just your business details — what you do, your contact info, any content/text you want on the site, and examples of websites you like. We handle the rest: design, development, hosting setup, and launch.
Do you offer revisions? ▼
Yes! Starter includes 1 round of revisions, Business includes 2 rounds. Additional revisions are ₹500 each. We want you to be happy with the final product.
What's included in the price? ▼
Everything needed to go live: design, development, mobile responsiveness, contact form, basic SEO, and deployment to a live server. Domain and hosting are separate (we can help you set those up).
Do you provide hosting? ▼
We can set up hosting for you on reliable platforms. We recommend and can manage hosting starting at ₹1,500/year. We'll guide you through the best option for your needs.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept UPI, bank transfer, and PayPal. 50% advance payment to start, 50% on completion. For retainer plans, monthly billing via UPI or bank transfer.
Can you help with an existing website? ▼
Absolutely! We can redesign, add features, fix issues, or optimize your existing website. Share your current site and what you'd like changed, and we'll provide a quote.
What makes Vibe Coder different from other web agencies? ▼
Three things: (1) We're AI-native — we use AI tools to deliver faster and at better prices. (2) We're design-first — every site looks premium, not template-y. (3) We're founder-led — you work directly with the person building your project, not a project manager.
