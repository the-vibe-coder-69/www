Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 Is this really free?

Yes. No hidden fees, no credit card required. We do this because (a) it builds goodwill in the local business community, and (b) if you like our work, you might hire us to fix things. But there's zero obligation.

🔹 How long does the audit take?

We deliver the audit report within 48 hours of receiving your request. It's comprehensive but fast.

🔹 What if I find a bug on my own site?

Report it through this page! We love catching bugs — and if it's a genuine security issue, we'll fix it for free as part of our community goodwill program.

🔹 Are there any limits?

We offer 20 free audits per month to keep quality high. Each audit covers one website. If you need multiple sites, we can discuss a custom plan.

🔹 Will you try to upsell me?

The audit report stands on its own — it's genuinely useful whether or not you hire us. The report will mention "we can fix these for you" but there's no sales pitch, no follow-up calls, no pressure.
