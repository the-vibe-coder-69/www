📖 Client Guide

What to Expect When You Hire Us

A transparent walkthrough of our entire engagement — from your first message to launch day and beyond.

1. Getting Started

You've made the smart decision to level up your digital presence. Here's what happens next:

  1. You reach out — via WhatsApp, email, or our contact form. Tell us about your project.
  2. We respond within 2 hours — during business hours. Usually faster.
  3. Quick discovery call — 15-20 minutes on WhatsApp or phone to understand your needs.
  4. We send a proposal — clear scope, timeline, and fixed price. No hidden fees.
  5. You approve & we start — 50% advance, and we're off to the races.

💡 Pro Tip

Before reaching out, have a rough idea of what you want — but don't worry if you're unsure. We'll help you figure it out. The more you share, the more accurate our quote will be.

2. During the Project

Once we start, here's how we keep you in the loop:

  • Weekly updates — every Monday, you get a status update with screenshots/video of progress.
  • Open WhatsApp channel — you can message us anytime with questions or feedback.
  • Live preview — we'll share a link where you can see the work in progress.
  • Revision rounds — your package includes a set number of revision rounds. Use them!
  • No surprises — if something will take longer or cost more, we tell you before it happens, not after.

3. What We Need From You

To deliver the best results, here's what helps us help you:

  • Business information — what you do, your target audience, your unique value
  • Content & copy — text, images, logos, brand colors you'd like to use
  • References — 2-3 websites you like (even competitors), tell us what you love about them
  • Timely feedback — the faster you review and respond, the faster we deliver
  • Domain/hosting access — if you already have these, we'll need access to set things up

Don't have all of this ready? No problem. We can help you create content, source images, and set up everything from scratch.

4. Delivery & Launch

When everything is polished and tested, we prepare for launch:

  • Final review — you get the final version on a staging URL for one last look
  • QA sign-off — we run our full Website QA Checklist before anything goes live
  • Deployment — we handle hosting setup, domain connection, SSL, and going live
  • Final payment — 50% balance payment upon completion and your approval
  • Handover — you get all files, logins, and a quick walkthrough of how to manage things

🎯 Our Promise

We don't consider a project done until you're genuinely happy. If something isn't right, we fix it. No arguing, no extra charges for reasonable revisions within scope.

5. After Launch

Our relationship doesn't end when your site goes live:

  • 30-day bug fix window — if anything breaks, we fix it free of charge
  • Priority support — existing clients get faster responses
  • Monthly maintenance plans — available from ₹5,000/month for updates, security, and content changes
  • Referral program — refer a friend and get 10% off your next project
  • We stay in touch — not in a creepy way. Just occasional check-ins to see how things are going.

6. Pricing Philosophy

We believe in transparent, value-based pricing:

  • Fixed price projects — no hourly billing. You know the cost upfront.
  • 50/50 payment — 50% to start, 50% on completion. Fair for both sides.
  • No hidden costs — domain registration, hosting, and third-party tools are extra, and we tell you exactly what they cost before you commit.
  • Value, not cheapness — we're not the cheapest, but we're the best value. You get premium quality at Indian prices.

Quick FAQs

How long does a typical website take?

Starter websites: 3-5 days. Business websites: 5-7 days. Custom apps: 2-4 weeks. We'll give you a precise timeline in the proposal.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, bank transfer, and PayPal. We're flexible.

Do I need to have content ready?

It helps, but it's not required. We can guide you on what to prepare, or help create content for an additional fee.

What if I don't like the design?

We include revision rounds in every package. We'll keep iterating until you're happy — that's our policy.

Do you provide hosting?

We set up hosting for you and can manage it. Hosting costs start at ₹1,500/year.
